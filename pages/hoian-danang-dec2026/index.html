HOI AN · LANTERNS & OLD TOWN
DAY 01WEDNESDAY · 02 DECEMBER

Arrival in Vietnam & a lantern-lit Hoi An evening

Da Nang Airport → Little Oasis Hoi An → French House → Ancient Town

13:25

Arrive at Da Nang International Airport

Land on Philippine Airlines flight PR585 from Manila. Once immigration and baggage collection are complete, meet the private driver and transfer to Hoi An.

AFTERNOON

Check in & unwind at Little Oasis Hoi An

Settle into the hotel, freshen up and enjoy a relaxed afternoon before heading into the lantern-filled Old Town.

EVENING

Vietnamese set-menu dinner at French House

Enjoy a pre-arranged Vietnamese dinner showcasing familiar local flavors in a comfortable restaurant setting.

Planned tour meal
AFTER DINNER

Hoi An Ancient Town, lantern boat & night market

Walk through the charming old streets after dark, take a traditional lantern boat ride along the Hoai River and release floating lanterns. Continue to the night market for colorful lantern photographs and a leisurely stroll.

Planned evening experience
HOI AN · COCONUT FOREST & HERITAGE
DAY 02THURSDAY · 03 DECEMBER

Basket boats, ancient houses & Hoi An flavors

Cam Thanh Coconut Forest → Hoi An Ancient Town → Banh mi experience

TWO POSSIBLE DEPARTURE TIMES · SAME TOUR ROUTE
10:00 AMMorning departureExplore earlier and enjoy banh mi around lunchtime.
2:00 PMAfternoon departureA free morning before setting off on the same tour.

Both timings follow the same route below. The departure time is to be coordinated with the group.

PICK-UP

Hotel pick-up & transfer to Cam Thanh

Leave Little Oasis Hoi An for the peaceful coconut waterways outside the historic town.

FIRST STOP

Cam Thanh Coconut Forest – basket boat ride

Glide through water-coconut palms in a traditional round bamboo basket boat. See a different side of Hoi An through its local river culture and lush scenery.

NEXT

Discover Hoi An's heritage quarter

Walk the UNESCO-listed Ancient Town and visit highlights such as Chinese assembly halls, centuries-old merchant houses and the Japanese Covered Bridge (Chua Cau). Enjoy the architectural details and stories of a former trading port.

FOOD STOP

Taste a Hoi An-style banh mi

Pause for one of the town's best-loved street-food specialties: a crisp Vietnamese baguette filled with fresh herbs and flavorful toppings. This is a light local-food stop rather than a full multi-dish food tour.

EVENING

Free evening at leisure

Enjoy the hotel, shop for souvenirs or explore Hoi An independently. No fixed dinner reservation is planned for this evening.

At leisure
DA NANG · COAST, CULTURE & NIGHT LIGHTS
DAY 03FRIDAY · 04 DECEMBER

From Hoi An to the best of Da Nang

Marble Mountains → Han Market → Pizza 4P's → Son Tra → Madame Lan → Han River cruise

10:30

Check out from Little Oasis Hoi An

Meet your driver and begin a scenic transfer north to Da Nang, with sightseeing stops along the way.

LATE MORNING

Marble Mountains & traditional stone carving

Explore the striking limestone hills of Ngu Hanh Son, known for caves, pagodas, beautiful stone steps and viewpoints. Stop by the local stone-carving village to admire the area's traditional craftsmanship.

There are stairs and uneven paths in the mountain area; the visit can be paced to suit the group.
BEFORE LUNCH

Shopping at Han Market

Browse this lively central market for souvenirs, Vietnamese snacks, local specialties and gifts.

~13:30

Lunch at Pizza 4P's – Indochina

Take a lunch break at Pizza 4P's, known for its pizza and creative pasta dishes. Advance table booking is recommended.

Meal at your own expense
AFTER LUNCH

Son Tra Marina – seaside coffee stop

Relax at this photogenic waterfront café on the Son Tra Peninsula, with white-and-blue architecture and views across the bay.

Drinks at your own expense
AFTERNOON

Linh Ung Pagoda & the 67-meter Lady Buddha

Visit one of Da Nang's most scenic spiritual landmarks, set against mountain greenery and sweeping ocean views. Admire the majestic 67-meter Lady Buddha (Guanyin) statue and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the pagoda grounds.

LATE AFTERNOON

Check in at Nesta Hotel Da Nang

Arrive at the Da Nang hotel and take a break before the evening program.

18:30

Hotel pick-up for dinner at Madame Lan

Head to Madame Lan Restaurant for a Vietnamese dinner in a distinctive setting. Dinner costs are not yet specified as included.

Meal cost to be confirmed
~20:00

Han River night cruise

Board an evening cruise to see Da Nang's illuminated waterfront, bridges and skyline from the river. The exact boarding time and cruise operator will be confirmed.

21:00

Dragon Bridge fire & water show

Watch the Dragon Bridge's spectacular fire-and-water performance, subject to weather and the confirmed cruise route. The usual Friday performance begins at 9:00 PM—not at 8:00 PM.

Cruise timing & ticket arrangements to be confirmed

The daytime sequence is ambitious. Shopping time, the mountain visit and coffee stop may be adjusted to keep the 13:30 lunch booking and evening appointments comfortable.

BA NA HILLS · ABOVE THE CLOUDS
DAY 04SATURDAY · 05 DECEMBER

A day in the clouds at Ba Na Hills

Golden Bridge → Flower Gardens → French Village → Moon Castle

08:30

Depart Nesta Hotel Da Nang

Meet your driver for the journey into the mountains to Ba Na Hills, a high-altitude attraction with panoramic views and a wide range of themed experiences.

MORNING

Golden Bridge & flower gardens

Walk across the famous Golden Bridge, held aloft by enormous sculpted hands, and explore the beautiful Le Jardin d'Amour flower gardens. Visibility at the bridge depends on mountain weather.

MIDDAY

Buffet lunch at Four Seasons Restaurant

Take a break and enjoy a buffet lunch during your Ba Na Hills visit.

Planned tour meal
AFTERNOON

French Village & Moon Castle

Explore the European-inspired squares and buildings of the French Village, then continue to Moon Castle, subject to operating schedules and access on the day.

LATE AFTERNOON

Return to Da Nang

Travel back to the city and enjoy some time to rest at Nesta Hotel.

EVENING

Seafood dinner in Da Nang

Enjoy fresh local seafood at a Da Nang seafood restaurant. The restaurant selection and dinner payment arrangement will be confirmed separately.

Dinner arrangements to be confirmed

Ba Na Hills entrance tickets are part of the planned tour services. Some attractions may change operation due to weather or maintenance.

DA NANG · A DAY AT YOUR OWN PACE

Free day to relax & explore

Beach time · Café hopping · Shopping · Personal discoveries

ALL DAY

Free & easy in Da Nang

Enjoy the day entirely at your own pace. Sleep in, unwind at the hotel, spend time by the beach, explore cafés or discover local neighborhoods independently. There is no fixed group tour or pre-arranged meal today.

Free time · At your own expense

Browse the shops at Han Market, admire the pink Da Nang Cathedral, try local seafood noodles, stop at Boulevard for gelato or bingsu, or visit the vintage-style Nam House Coffee.

These are suggestions, not scheduled or included tour stops. If private transport or a guided outing is requested, it can be arranged separately.
