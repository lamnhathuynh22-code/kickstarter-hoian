THE JOURNEY

Six days of discovery.

Open each day to see the details. Your optional experiences are marked throughout.

HOI AN
DAY 01 · WEDNESDAY, DEC 02

Welcome to Vietnam

Airport arrival · Slow afternoon · A magical evening

13:25Arrive at Da Nang International Airport on Philippine Airlines PR585. Meet your private driver after immigration and transfer to Hoi An.

AFTERNOONCheck in at Little Oasis Hoi An and enjoy time to rest.

EVENINGVietnamese set-menu dinner at French House Restaurant, followed by an evening activity of your choice: lantern boat on the Hoai River or Hoi An Memories Show (20:00–21:00).

✦ Evening selection requested
CULTURE & FOOD
DAY 02 · THURSDAY, DEC 03

A Taste of Hoi An

Your morning · Old Town stroll · Local flavors

MORNINGFree time to unwind. Or discover Hoi An's famous custom tailoring and leather goods, or join a hands-on leather craft workshop (approximately 2 hours). Tailoring orders may be fitted later in the day and collected the next morning, subject to the shop's timing.

15:00Explore Hoi An Ancient Town on a combined city walk and mini food tour: white rose dumplings, fried wontons, cao lau and banh mi at Madam Khanh – The Banh Mi Queen. Try coconut coffee or salted coffee and visit heritage highlights.

EVENINGFree time for shopping and enjoying the old town. If not experienced on Dec 02, the lantern boat or Hoi An Memories Show may be enjoyed tonight instead, subject to timing and tickets. No separate dinner is scheduled after the food tour.

✦ Morning & evening selections requested
NATURE & HERITAGE
DAY 03 · FRIDAY, DEC 04

From Hoi An to Da Nang

Marble Mountains · Son Tra Peninsula · Coastal city

12:00Check out of Little Oasis Hoi An and depart by private vehicle for Da Nang.

DAYTIMEDiscover Marble Mountains, traditional stone-carving works and historic pagodas. Continue to Son Tra Peninsula and Linh Ung Pagoda, home to the 67-meter Lady Buddha statue. Enjoy lunch at a local Da Nang restaurant; exact timing and stop order will be finalized.

LATERCheck in at Nesta Hotel Da Nang.

EVENINGFree time. Ideas: AEON Mall shopping, dinner at Bep Cuon, Madame Lan, a seafood restaurant, or Pizza 4P's (reservation recommended), then browse Son Tra Night Market and see the Dragon Bridge and Han River after dark.

Evening suggestions · At leisure
MOUNTAIN ESCAPE
DAY 04 · SATURDAY, DEC 05

A Day Above the Clouds

Ba Na Hills · Mountain scenery · Buffet lunch

08:30Depart from the hotel by private vehicle for Ba Na Hills.

DAYTIMEDiscover the mountain resort and its highlights, including the famous Golden Bridge, subject to site access and weather. Enjoy buffet lunch at Four Seasons Restaurant.

EVENINGReturn to Da Nang and enjoy the evening at leisure.

Planned excursion
YOUR PACE
DAY 05 · SUNDAY, DEC 06

Da Nang, Your Way

Rest & recharge · Or uncover a local side of the city

ALL DAYEnjoy a free relaxing day at your own pace, or opt for a local city and food experience.

OPTIONALShop at Han Market, visit Da Nang Cathedral, try local seafood noodles and stop for gelato or bingsu at Boulevard or retro-inspired Vietnamese coffee at Nam House Coffee.

✦ Optional tour selection requested
