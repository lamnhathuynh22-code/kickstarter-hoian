SON TRA · MARBLE MOUNTAINS
DAY 02WEDNESDAY · 23 DECEMBER

Guanyin, the Five Elements & the Coast

Son Tra → Marble Mountains → Dragon Bridge & My Khe
08:30

Hotel pick-up · Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra

Begin the morning in the quiet grounds of the seaside Linh Ung Pagoda. The 67-metre Lady Buddha represents Guanyin (觀音), the bodhisattva of compassion — a familiar figure across many Mahayana Buddhist traditions. Take in the mountain greenery and wide coastal views.

MORNING

Marble Mountains · Five Elements (五行)

Continue to the cluster of five limestone hills named after Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth. Explore Linh Ung Non Nuoc, the older pagoda in Da Nang's trio of Linh Ung temples, and the intimate Tang Chon Cave, where Buddhist worship and regional spiritual traditions meet.

MORNING

Non Nuoc stone-carving village

See the local craft tradition of sculpting stone, including Buddhist imagery, guardian figures and ornamental works passed down through generations.

BEFORE 12:30

Dragon Bridge & My Khe Beach photo stops

Return toward central Da Nang for quick photographs at the Dragon Bridge and along the celebrated My Khe coastline.

~12:30

End of scheduled sightseeing

Free afternoon and evening at your own pace.

OPTIONAL LUNCH

Madame Lân Restaurant — a welcoming introduction to Vietnamese regional dishes, if you would like us to arrange a table.

Pacing note: This is an ambitious half-day route. Photo stops or time at individual sites may be shortened depending on traffic and walking pace. Marble Mountains have steps and uneven surfaces.

HOI AN · LIVING HERITAGE
DAY 05SATURDAY · 26 DECEMBER

From Coconut Palms to the Old Trading Port

Da Nang check-out → Cam Thanh → Hoi An
MORNING

Check-out & Cam Thanh basket boat ride

Leave Da Nang and travel through Cam Thanh's water-coconut groves aboard a traditional round basket boat — a lively, distinctly local river experience.

FLEXIBLE

Optional Hoi An culinary tasting

Discover a few well-loved local specialties at established eateries known for preparing them. Portions can be kept intentionally small so the experience feels like a tasting rather than a heavy meal.

OPTIONAL FOOD EXPERIENCE
White Rose DumplingsDelicate steamed rice dumplings with Chinese culinary influence, adapted into a Hoi An specialty with a local dipping sauce.
Cao Lau NoodlesA distinctive Hoi An noodle dish, closely associated with the old trading town.
Hoi An Banh MiA crisp baguette filled with local ingredients and signature sauces.

AFTERNOON

Ancient Town heritage walk

Explore historic Chinese assembly halls, centuries-old merchant houses and the Japanese Covered Bridge. These sites tell the story of Hoi An as a meeting place for Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese and other trading communities.

EVENING

Hoi An by lantern light

As night falls, return to the atmospheric streets, glowing lanterns and riverside ambience of the old town. Dinner is free at your own choice.
